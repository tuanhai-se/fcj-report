### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment at FCJ is welcoming, collaborative, and supportive. Team members are approachable and always willing to provide assistance whenever I face challenges, even beyond regular working hours. In addition, the office is clean, organized, and comfortable, enabling me to work efficiently and stay focused.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
My mentor consistently provided clear guidance and patiently answered my questions whenever I encountered difficulties. Rather than offering immediate solutions, my mentor encouraged me to think critically and solve problems independently, which greatly enhanced my learning experience. The team administrator also offered prompt support with administrative tasks and documentation, ensuring a smooth internship process.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The tasks assigned during my internship closely aligned with my academic studies while exposing me to technologies and concepts beyond the classroom. This experience strengthened my existing knowledge and allowed me to apply theoretical concepts in practical situations, improving both my technical competence and confidence.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
DThe internship provided numerous opportunities to develop both technical and professional skills. I improved my understanding of cloud technologies, gained experience working in a collaborative team environment, and enhanced my communication skills in a professional workplace. Additionally, my mentor shared practical industry knowledge and career advice that broadened my perspective on future career development.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
FCJ promotes a friendly, respectful, and collaborative working culture. Team members communicate openly, support one another, and work together effectively, especially during demanding projects. This positive environment made me feel like a valued member of the team despite being an intern.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The company provides internship allowances and offers flexible working arrangements, allowing interns to balance their work responsibilities with their academic commitments. These policies create a supportive environment that encourages both learning and productivity.

### Suggestions & Expectations

One aspect I appreciated most was the flexible working schedule, which allowed me to choose and register my office working days according to my availability.

If given the opportunity, I would highly recommend this internship to students pursuing a similar field. In addition to strengthening academic knowledge, interns gain valuable hands-on experience with cloud computing and develop practical skills that are highly relevant to the IT industry.
